FinAssist

An offline-first household finance and obligation tracker for Android.

What it does

Tracks bills, loans, insurance, assets, family important dates, health checkups, and medications in one place — designed to work fully offline, with your data stored on your own device.

Backup

FinAssist can optionally back up your data to a private, hidden folder in your own Google Drive that only this app can access. Nothing is shared with any third party. See the privacy policy for details.

Who this is for

FinAssist is a personal project, shared directly with family and friends rather than distributed through the Play Store.
