Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-29

FinAssist ("the app") is a personal finance and household obligation tracker built for offline use on your own Android device. This page explains what data the app handles and how.

Data stored on your device

All of your data — obligations, transactions, assets, insurance policies, family members, and everything else you enter — is stored locally in a database on your own phone. The app works fully offline and does not send this data to any server operated by the developer.

Optional Google Drive backup

If you choose to connect Google Drive from the Backup & Restore screen:

  • The app requests access only to its own hidden, app-specific folder in your Google Drive (the drive.appdata scope) — not your general Drive files.
  • This hidden folder is not visible in your normal Drive file list and cannot be read by any other app.
  • A single backup file (a copy of the app's local database) is uploaded there, either on demand or on a daily schedule if you enable automatic backup.
  • Only your own Google account can access this backup. The developer has no access to it.
  • You can disconnect Google Drive at any time from the Backup & Restore screen, which stops all future uploads.

What the app does not do

  • It does not share, sell, or transmit your data to any third party.
  • It does not use your data for advertising or analytics.
  • It does not access any Google Drive files outside its own app-specific folder.

Contact

This is a personal, non-commercial project shared directly with family and friends. If you have questions about this policy, please contact the person who shared the app with you.
